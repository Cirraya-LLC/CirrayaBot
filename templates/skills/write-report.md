# Writing a report

Structure:
1. **Summary** — three to five sentences: what was asked, what you found, what you recommend. Someone who reads only
   this should know what to do.
2. **Findings** — one short section each, most important first. Facts, with where they came from.
3. **Recommendation** — what to do next, and why. If there are options, the trade-off of each.
4. **Open questions** — what you don't know yet and how it could be found out.

Rules:
- Plain words, short sentences. No filler, no "in conclusion".
- Numbers with their units and dates. Quotes with their source.
- Mark clearly what is your opinion and what is a fact.

Save it as Markdown in `/work/reports/<short-name>-<yyyy-mm-dd>.md` and hand it over with `send_file`.
